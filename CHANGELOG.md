# LFG Filter Forever

## 1.3.0

- First public release.
- Players: level range, roles (Tank / Healer / Damage) and classes.
- Groups: Has Tank and Has Healer (any / has one / has none), Has DPS Spot.
- Popover on the Looking For Group gear button, with Blizzard's Show All Level Ranges option
  kept at the top. The red X on the gear resets every filter.
- Hidden players and groups are counted under the list.
- Localised in every client language.
